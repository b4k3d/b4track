B4TRACK Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

B4TRACK is an open-source privacy tool maintained by b4k3d. This policy explains what information the B4TRACK Android application and website handle.

Summary





B4TRACK does not require an account.



B4TRACK does not include analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.



URL cleaning is performed locally on your device.



URLs and clipboard contents are not sent to B4TRACK servers for normal cleaning.



Local history and settings remain on your device.

Information processed on your device

B4TRACK may process the following information locally when you use the app:





URLs or text that you paste, type, select, share, or submit for cleaning



Clipboard text when you use clipboard sanitizing or enable automatic clipboard cleaning



Cleaned-link history, if history is created by the app



App settings such as theme, aggressive mode, auto-copy, and clipboard-cleaning preferences

This information is stored locally by Android and is not uploaded to B4TRACK by the app's normal cleaning functions.

Clipboard access

B4TRACK only monitors the clipboard when you explicitly enable Clean copied URLs automatically. Android displays an ongoing foreground-service notification while this feature is enabled.

When enabled, B4TRACK checks copied text locally for URLs. If tracking parameters are found, the app replaces the clipboard text with the cleaned version. Clipboard contents are not transmitted to B4TRACK servers.

You can disable this feature at any time in Settings. You can also clear the app's local history or uninstall the app to remove locally stored app data.

Sharing and external apps

When you use Android's share menu, B4TRACK receives the text selected for cleaning. If you choose to copy or share the result, Android passes that text to the app you select. That receiving app's own privacy policy applies after the text leaves B4TRACK.

B4TRACK may open external links such as:





B4TRACK website



B4TRACK GitHub repository



Buy Me a Coffee

Those websites and services operate independently and have their own policies.

Network access and custom rules

The standard B4TRACK cleaning flow is designed to work on-device without sending URLs to a remote server.

The app includes support for user-created JavaScript cleaning rules. A custom rule may request network data if you choose to install or run a rule that uses network access. B4TRACK does not control or guarantee the behavior of third-party or user-created rules. Review custom rules before enabling them.

The app also requires Internet access for normal Android and external-link functionality. B4TRACK does not use that permission to upload your normal cleaning history or clipboard contents.

Data sharing

B4TRACK does not sell, rent, or share your personal information. The app has no B4TRACK account system, analytics backend, advertising network, or URL-tracking service.

Data may leave the app only when you intentionally:





Share cleaned text with another Android app



Open an external website



Use a custom rule that makes a network request

Data retention and deletion

B4TRACK keeps local history and preferences on your device until you remove them. You can clear history from the History screen. Uninstalling B4TRACK removes its local app data according to Android's normal app-uninstall behavior.

B4TRACK does not maintain a server-side copy of your cleaned links, clipboard contents, or local history.

Children's privacy

B4TRACK does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from anyone else.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when B4TRACK's functionality or privacy practices change. The latest version will be published in this file and in the B4TRACK source repository.
