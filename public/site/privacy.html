Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 20, 2026

TL;DR

B4TRACK collects zero data. Everything happens locally on your device. Nothing is ever sent to any server.

Overview

B4TRACK is a URL tracking parameter remover built on one principle: your data never leaves your device. This policy explains what that means in practice.

Information We Do NOT Collect

  • ✕URLs you paste or clean
  • ✕Browsing history or clipboard content
  • ✕Personal information of any kind
  • ✕Device identifiers or analytics
  • ✕Crash reports or usage telemetry

Local Storage Only

Your cleaning history and settings are stored exclusively in your device's localStorage. This data never leaves your device and is never transmitted anywhere. You can clear it anytime from the app's History tab.

Permissions

  • → Internet — Used solely to load the app itself. No user data is transmitted.
  • → Clipboard — Read only when you tap "Paste" or "Sanitize Clipboard." Never stored or sent anywhere.

No Third-Party Services

B4TRACK does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking SDKs, or data brokers. There are no external network requests made during normal operation.

Google Play Data Safety

This app complies with Google Play's data safety requirements. No user data is collected, shared, or transmitted to any server. All processing occurs entirely on-device.

Children's Privacy

B4TRACK does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13. Since no data is collected at all, COPPA compliance is inherent.

Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at b4track.app/privacy with a new effective date.
